Legal process outsourcing (LPO) for U.S. law firms and in-house legal teams: dedicated paralegal and
legal-assistant professionals based in India, working your U.S. hours. They support family law,
personal injury and medical, civil and criminal litigation, immigration, bankruptcy, and real estate

> **Important:** Akrostech is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. All work is

This is the heart of the service. Every engagement includes: signed NDAs; role-based access controls
and least-privilege logins; matter-level confidentiality protocols; secure handling of PII and
PHI-adjacent records; and a written supervision structure naming the responsible attorney. We train
on your ethical obligations as well as our own — and we put it all in the contract before work
begins.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is legal process outsourcing?

LPO is delegated legal-support work — intake, document prep, records, discovery, calendaring —
performed by trained paralegal professionals under attorney supervision. It gives firms extra
capacity without the cost of additional in-house staff.

### Is it ethical for a law firm to outsource paralegal work?

Yes, when done correctly. The ABA Model Rules permit lawyers to delegate to non-lawyer assistants
with proper supervision, and outsourcing — domestic or offshore — is widely practiced. We build the
supervision and confidentiality structure with you upfront, in writing.

### How do you protect client confidentiality?

NDAs, access controls, least-privilege logins, matter-level protocols, and secure handling
procedures — agreed in writing before any matter is touched. Ask us for the full protocol document.

### Who supervises the work?

Your attorneys. Every substantive output is prepared for attorney review, and nothing goes to a
client, court, or opposing counsel without it. We name the supervising attorney in the engagement.

### Do you provide legal advice?

No. Akrostech is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. We provide paralegal and
legal-assistant support under attorney supervision only.

### Which case management systems do you work in?

Clio, MyCase, Filevine, Smokeball, and PracticePanther — plus your document and billing tools. We
train on your templates and workflows.
