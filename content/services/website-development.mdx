# Website development for small businesses that need it done right

{/* HERO: eyebrow="Website Development", H1 above, lead below, CTAs: "Book a 30-min call" / "See packages", visual: Live Build (code editor ↔ browser frame) */}

Akrostech is a design-led web studio with the same dedicated-team model as our staffing services.
You get a marketing site, a web app, or an online store — designed around your customers, built on
modern tech, and fast enough to rank. Buy a fixed-scope project, or keep a dedicated developer or
designer on your hours, every week.

## What you get, plainly

Akrostech designs and builds websites and web apps for U.S. startups, small businesses, agencies,
and law and accounting firms. Every build includes UX design, technical SEO, performance and
accessibility work, and hosting setup — then a care plan keeps it updated after launch. You can buy
a one-time project or a dedicated developer who works as part of your team.

## The problems we solve

**Before:** Your site looks five years old, loads slowly, and buries the contact button. You dread
sending prospects to it.

**After:** A site you're proud to link. It loads in under two seconds, says what you do in the first
screen, and turns visitors into booked calls.

**Before:** Your last developer vanished mid-project and nobody can explain the codebase.

**After:** You own everything — code, designs, logins. Weekly demos keep you in control, and
documentation means any developer can pick it up.

**Before:** Your WordPress site breaks every time a plugin updates.

**After:** A modern stack with updates tested before they ship, plus a care plan that handles the
boring parts for you.

## What's included

- **Marketing websites** — up to large multi-page sites with CMS editing
- **Custom web apps** — dashboards, portals, booking flows, internal tools
- **E-commerce** — Shopify builds and custom storefronts with checkout done right
- **Landing pages** — focused pages for campaigns, tuned for conversion
- **Redesigns and migrations** — from Wix, Squarespace, or legacy WordPress
- **UI/UX design** — wireframes to polished visual design, in your brand
- **Technical SEO** — semantic markup, metadata, sitemaps, structured data
- **Performance and accessibility** — Core Web Vitals budgets, WCAG 2.2 AA
- **Headless and WordPress CMS** — Sanity, Contentful, or WordPress you can actually edit
- **Hosting setup** — Vercel or AWS, configured and documented
- **Monthly care plans** — updates, monitoring, backups, small changes

## How it works

1. **Discover.** We map your goals, customers, and content in a working session. You get a sitemap,
   a scope doc, and a fixed quote.
2. **Design.** Wireframes first, then visual design in your brand. You approve every screen before
   code starts.
3. **Build.** We build in weekly sprints with a staging link you can open any time.
4. **Test.** We check every page on real phones and browsers, run accessibility and performance
   audits, and fix what fails.
5. **Launch.** We handle DNS, hosting, analytics, and search-console setup. Launch day is boring on
   purpose.
6. **Grow.** A care plan keeps the site updated, monitored, and improving — or hand the keys to your
   team with full docs.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does a small business website cost?

It depends on scope, not vibes. A focused marketing site (our Launch package) costs less than a
CMS-driven content site (Growth), which costs less than a custom web app (Product). `{{CONFIRM}}`
package pricing. Every quote is fixed and tied to a written scope — the price we sign is the price
you pay.

### How long does a website build take?

A Launch site typically takes 3–5 weeks from kickoff to go-live; Growth sites run 6–10 weeks;
Product builds are scoped in phases. `{{CONFIRM}}` current timelines. You'll see progress weekly on a
staging link, so there are no surprises at the end.

### Will I be able to edit the site myself?

Yes. Every content site ships with a CMS (Sanity, Contentful, or WordPress) and a recorded
walkthrough. If you can use Google Docs, you can update your pages. Code-level changes stay with us
or your developer.

### Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes — redesigns and migrations are a core offer. We audit what you have, keep what works (content,
rankings, URLs with proper redirects), and rebuild what doesn't.

### Who owns the code and designs?

You do. Repositories, design files, hosting, and analytics all transfer to accounts you control at
launch. We keep no hostages.

### What happens after launch?

Most clients move to a Care plan: updates, uptime monitoring, backups, and a monthly allowance of
small changes. Or take the keys — full documentation is included either way.

### Can I hire just a developer instead of a project?

Yes. Dedicated developers and designers work full-time or part-time, join your rituals, and work
your hours. It's the same model as our staffing services, applied to web work.

### Do you work with our in-house team or agency?

Happily. We slot into your process — your PM, your repo, your standups — or run the whole thing and
report weekly. Your call.
