# Bookkeeping and accounting support you can trust with the numbers

{/* HERO: eyebrow="Accounting Outsourcing", H1 above, visual: The Ledger Balances (split-flap figures → "Reconciled" stamp) */}

Your Akrostech accounting professional works inside your books every day: transactions categorized,
invoices sent and chased, bills paid on time, payroll processed, month-end closed on schedule. Small
businesses get a finance function without a finance hire. CPA firms get reliable capacity during tax
season and every month after.

## What you get, plainly

Dedicated outsourced bookkeeping and accounting support from India-based professionals working your
U.S. hours. The scope covers bookkeeping, accounts payable and receivable, invoicing and billing,
payroll processing, month-end close support, reconciliations, and 1099 and sales-tax prep support —
always alongside your CPA, never replacing them. We work in QuickBooks, Xero, or Zoho Books: your
chart of accounts, your rules.

## The problems we solve

**Before:** The books are three months behind. You find out about cash problems when the card
declines.

**After:** Books closed by the 10th, every month. You see cash position weekly and make decisions
with real numbers.

**Before:** Invoices go out late, get paid later, and nobody follows up.

**After:** Invoicing runs like clockwork, AR aging is reviewed weekly, and overdue accounts get
polite, persistent follow-up.

**Before:** Payroll is a stressful scramble every two weeks, and you're never quite sure the filings
are right.

**After:** Payroll processed on schedule, filings tracked, and your CPA gets clean records instead
of a shoebox in April.

## What's included

- **Bookkeeping** — daily transaction categorization and ledger maintenance
- **Accounts payable** — bill entry, approval workflows, scheduled payments
- **Accounts receivable** — invoicing, statements, collections follow-up
- **Payroll processing** — runs, filings calendar, year-end support
- **Month-end close support** — reconciliations, accruals, close checklist
- **Reconciliations** — bank, credit card, and balance-sheet accounts
- **1099 and sales-tax prep support** — organized records for your CPA to file
- **Management reports** — P&L, balance sheet, and cash-flow summaries monthly

## How it works

1. **Discover.** We review your books, chart of accounts, and pain points — free.
2. **Clean up.** If the books are behind, we scope a one-time cleanup first.
3. **Set the cadence.** Daily/weekly/monthly task lists, agreed with you and your CPA.
4. **Run.** Your dedicated accountant works the checklist; you get weekly summaries.
5. **Close.** Month-end closes on schedule with a review call.
6. **Report.** KPIs and exception reports keep you in control without the detail work.

## Engagement models

- **Dedicated full-time** — your accountant, 40 hrs/week, embedded in your finance flow.
- **Dedicated part-time** — steady monthly coverage for smaller transaction volumes.
- **Project-based** — cleanup projects and tax-season support with a defined end.

We're the engine room; your CPA stays the architect. We prepare clean, reconciled books and

### Will you replace my CPA?

No — we work alongside them. We handle the day-to-day books; your CPA handles tax strategy, filings,
and advice. Most CPAs love us: clean books make their job easier.

### How do you keep my financial data secure?

NDAs, access controls, least-privilege logins via a password manager, and confidentiality protocols
in the contract. We access only the systems you approve.

### What if my books are months behind?

We start with a scoped cleanup project — fixed price, defined end date — then move to steady monthly
support. You'll know the cleanup cost before we touch a transaction.

### Which accounting software do you support?

QuickBooks Online, Xero, and Zoho Books as primary platforms, plus payroll and expense tools around
them. If you're on desktop QuickBooks or something unusual, ask us.

### How fast is month-end close?

Our target is books closed by the 10th of the following month, with a review call. `{{CONFIRM}}`
current close timelines.

### Can you handle payroll and 1099s?

Yes — payroll processing with a filings calendar, and 1099/sales-tax prep support so your CPA files
from organized records.

### Do I get reports I can actually read?

Monthly P&L, balance sheet, and cash-flow summaries in plain language, plus a weekly cash-position
snapshot. No accounting degree required.
