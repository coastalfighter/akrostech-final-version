# A virtual assistant who actually takes work off your plate

{/* HERO: eyebrow="Virtual Assistance", H1 above, visual: The Inbox at Zero (chaotic notifications → sorted → "Inbox zero") */}

Your Akrostech virtual assistant is a dedicated professional — not a shared pool, not a task rabbit.
They learn your tools, your customers, and your preferences, then quietly absorb the work that's
been eating your week: inbox, calendar, follow-ups, data entry, customer messages, research. Most
clients get their first real week back within the first month.

## What you get, plainly

A dedicated virtual assistant (VA) from India who works for you alone, during your U.S. business
hours. They handle executive and admin support, inbox and calendar management, customer support
across chat/email/phone, social media and content support, CRM hygiene, data entry, and research.
You train them once on how you like things done; they run the playbook from there, with a weekly
review to keep quality tight.

## The problems we solve

**Before:** You start every day with 80 unread emails and end it having answered none of the
important ones.

**After:** Your inbox is at zero before you wake up. The urgent is flagged, the routine is handled,
and the noise is archived.

**Before:** Scheduling eats 45 minutes a day. Follow-ups slip. That proposal never got sent.

**After:** Your calendar runs itself. Meetings get booked, confirmations go out, and every follow-up
happens on time.

**Before:** You hired a VA from a marketplace before. They were juggling 12 clients and vanished
when it mattered.

**After:** One dedicated person, vetted for English and reliability, accountable to you — with a
backup trained on your account for sick days and vacations.

## What's included

- **Executive and admin support** — your right hand for the operational day-to-day
- **Inbox and calendar** — triage, drafting, scheduling, reminders
- **Customer support** — chat, email, and phone, in your voice
- **Social media management** — scheduling, engagement, basic reporting
- **Content support** — formatting, uploading, repurposing drafts
- **Data entry and CRM hygiene** — clean records, updated pipelines
- **Research** — lists, comparisons, summaries you can act on
- **Personal assistance** — travel, bookings, the life-admin that piles up

## How it works

1. **Discover.** A 30-minute call maps what eats your week and what "done" looks like.
2. **Match.** We shortlist vetted VAs against your tools, hours, and working style.
3. **Onboard.** Your VA trains on your systems and your playbook in the first week.
4. **Run.** Daily async updates plus a weekly review; you adjust priorities anytime.
5. **Scale.** Add hours, add a second VA, or specialize (customer support, bookkeeping prep) as you
   grow.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to hire a virtual assistant from India?

It depends on hours and skill mix — part-time starts well below the cost of a U.S. hire, and
full-time dedicated support costs a fraction of an in-house executive assistant. `{{CONFIRM}}` pricing

### Will my VA work during U.S. business hours?

Yes. Your VA's shift overlaps your workday across EST, CST, MST, and PST — with an overnight
"follow-the-sun" option if you want work done while you sleep.

### How is this different from hiring on a freelance marketplace?

Marketplace VAs juggle many clients and you manage the quality. Akrostech VAs are dedicated to you,
vetted for written and spoken English plus reliability, trained on your playbook, and backed by a
supervisor and a trained backup.

### What if my VA is sick or on vacation?

A backup VA, already briefed on your account, covers gaps. You never start a Monday wondering who's
handling your inbox.

### How do you handle security and confidentiality?

NDAs as standard, access controls on your accounts (password managers, least-privilege access), and
confidentiality protocols in the contract. This matters most for inboxes and customer data, and we
treat it that way.

### What tasks should I NOT give a VA?

Anything requiring licensed judgment (legal advice, tax filing decisions), and anything you haven't
defined "done" for yet. We'll tell you honestly when a task needs a specialist instead — that's what
our other services are for.

### How fast can we start?

Matching typically takes days, not weeks, and onboarding runs in the first week. `{{CONFIRM}}` exact
onboarding timeline.

### Can my VA talk to my customers directly?

Yes — by email, chat, or phone, in your voice, under your brand. We train on your tone and
escalation rules before they ever touch a customer.
