# Recruitment process outsourcing that fills your pipeline

{/* HERO: eyebrow="Recruitment Process Outsourcing", H1 above, visual: The Talent Funnel (avatar dots → stages → one glowing hire) */}

Akrostech's RPO service plugs a full recruiting team into your business. Dedicated recruiters
source, screen, and schedule — inside your ATS, on your hours, under your brand. Staffing agencies
use us to scale delivery without scaling headcount. HR teams use us to clear the requisition backlog
without burning out their in-house recruiters.

## What RPO is, plainly

Recruitment process outsourcing (RPO) means a dedicated external team runs some or all of your
recruiting: finding candidates, screening them, managing your applicant tracking system, scheduling
interviews, and supporting offers and onboarding. Unlike a staffing agency that fills one role for a
fee, an RPO partner works as your recruiting department — measured on hires, speed, and quality,
month after month.

## The problems we solve

**Before:** Requisitions sit open for months. Your best recruiter just quit, and the remaining team
is drowning in unreviewed resumes.

**After:** Every req has an owner. Candidates move through screening in days, hiring managers get
shortlists they trust, and time-to-fill drops.

**Before:** You're paying agency fees of 20–25% per hire and still doing half the work yourself.

**After:** A dedicated team at a predictable monthly cost, working your process inside your systems
— no per-placement markup.

**Before:** Sourcing for niche roles (a travel nurse in Boise, a controls engineer) eats entire
weeks with nothing to show.

**After:** Recruiters who source full-time, with playbooks per industry, feeding you vetted
shortlists on a cadence you set.

## How it works

1. **Discover.** We learn your roles, hiring bar, compensation bands, and process.
2. **Launch.** Your dedicated recruiters train on your ATS and employer brand.
3. **Source.** Multi-channel sourcing starts; you see first shortlists within days.
4. **Screen.** Structured screens against your knock-out criteria; only fits move forward.
5. **Hire.** We schedule, debrief, and support offers until acceptance.
6. **Improve.** Weekly reviews tune sourcing channels and screening bars from real data.

- **Dedicated recruiter** — full-time, works only on your reqs, joins your rituals.

- **Project-based** — a hiring surge with a defined end date (e.g., 20 hires in 90 days).
- **Modular** — hand us just sourcing or just scheduling; keep the rest in-house.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is recruitment process outsourcing?

RPO is when a dedicated external team runs part or all of your recruiting — sourcing, screening,
scheduling, and offer support — as an ongoing service. You get a recruiting department without
hiring one; you're measured on hires and speed, not hours.

### How is RPO different from using a staffing agency?

A staffing agency fills individual roles for a per-placement fee (often 20–25% of salary). RPO is a
dedicated team working your process month after month at a predictable cost. Agencies are
transactional; RPO is structural.

### Who owns the candidate relationships?

You do. We work inside your ATS, under your brand, and every candidate record, conversation, and
note stays in your systems.

### How fast can an RPO engagement start?

### Can you handle high-volume hiring?

Yes — sourcing pods scale to dozens of concurrent reqs, with daily throughput reporting so you
always know where every role stands.

### Do you recruit for niche or hard-to-fill roles?

That's the core use case: dedicated sourcers who do nothing but hunt for your hardest roles, with
industry-specific playbooks.

### How do you measure success?

Time-to-fill, time-to-shortlist, interview-to-offer ratio, and hiring-manager satisfaction —
reported weekly, reviewed monthly.
