# Built for your industry's reality

{/* HERO: Constellation — industry icons as stars joining into an "A". Clicking a star scrolls to its block. */}

Outsourcing fails when it's generic. A law firm's intake is nothing like a SaaS company's support
queue. Here's how each industry actually uses Akrostech — the roles, the workflows, the outcomes.

## Legal

Firms use **Legal Process Outsourcing** for intake, records retrieval, discovery support, and
calendaring — plus **Website Development** for intake-ready sites and **Virtual Assistance** for
phones and scheduling. Confidentiality protocols and attorney supervision are built in, not bolted
on.

## Healthcare

Practices and providers use **Virtual Assistance** for scheduling, insurance follow-ups, and admin —
and **RPO** for the eternal search for nurses and allied-health staff. HIPAA-aware handling
procedures apply. `{{CONFIRM}}` compliance details.

## Staffing

Staffing agencies use **RPO** as their delivery engine: dedicated sourcers and recruiters working
inside Bullhorn, JobDiva, or Ceipal — scaling placements without scaling headcount.

## E-commerce

Brands use **Website Development** (Shopify builds, conversion work), **Virtual Assistance**
(customer support, listings, order admin), and **Accounting Outsourcing** (bookkeeping that keeps up
with SKU chaos).

## SaaS

SaaS teams use **Website Development** (marketing sites and web apps), **Virtual Assistance**
(customer success support, documentation upkeep), and **RPO** (scaling engineering and GTM hiring).

## Real estate

Brokerages and investors use **Virtual Assistance** (lead follow-up, listing coordination,
transaction admin) and **Website Development** (sites with real listing integrations).
