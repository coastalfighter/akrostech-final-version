## Engagement models

### Dedicated full-time

One professional, 40 hours a week, working only for you. They join your rituals, report to you, and
ramp like an employee — without the payroll, benefits, or recruiting cost.

### Part-time

20 hours a week of dedicated support. The most popular starting point for founders and small teams.

### Project-based

A defined scope with a defined end: a website build, a bookkeeping cleanup, a hiring surge, a
discovery project. Fixed price, fixed timeline.

### Scalable team

A pod — e.g., three recruiters, or a developer plus a designer plus a VA — that grows or shrinks
with your roadmap. One contract, one review cadence.

## What affects the price

- **Role and seniority** — a senior developer costs more than a VA; both cost less than their U.S.
  equivalents.
- **Hours** — full-time, part-time, or project scope.
- **Volume** — teams price better per seat than solo engagements.

What never changes the price mid-stream: we quote fixed, in writing, before work starts.
