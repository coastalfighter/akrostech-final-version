Your day ends. Ours continues.

**6 PM, Austin.** You log off. Your Akrostech team in India starts its day with your brief.

**Overnight.** 12 candidates screened. Invoices reconciled. Homepage deployed. Support inbox
cleared.

**8 AM, Austin.** You wake up to finished work, not a to-do list.

That's the model: real-time overlap during your day, plus a follow-the-sun shift that hands finished
