## Getting started

### How fast can we start?

Matching takes days; onboarding runs in the first week. `{{CONFIRM}}` exact timeline.

### What does it cost?

Per-resource or per-project, quoted fixed before work starts — typically a fraction of a comparable
U.S. hire. `{{CONFIRM}}` pricing bands. [Estimate your savings](/pricing).

### Who owns the work and the data?

You do. Records, code, candidate data, and credentials stay in your systems under your control.

### Do you work during U.S. business hours?

Yes — overlap across EST, CST, MST, and PST, plus an overnight follow-the-sun option.

## Trust and security

### How do you vet professionals?

Three gates: domain skill (tested), written and spoken English, and reliability (references and
track record). `{{CONFIRM}}` vetting details.

### Is my data secure?

NDAs as standard, role-based access controls, least-privilege logins via password manager, and
confidentiality protocols in every contract — deepest for legal and accounting work.

### What happens if someone is sick or leaves?

Trained backups cover absences; replacements are matched and onboarded without you restarting from
zero.

## Working together

### How do we communicate?

Daily async updates in your Slack or Teams, a weekly 30-minute review, and monthly KPI reviews. Your
person is reachable during your business hours.

### Can we start with one service and add more?

That's the most common path. Most clients start with their biggest pain point and expand once the
model proves itself.

### What if it's not working?

Weekly reviews catch drift early. If a match isn't right, we re-match — the engagement is built to
be adjusted, not endured.

## Website Development

### How much does a small business website cost?

Scoped, not guessed: Launch < Growth < Product in cost, each fixed-quoted in writing. `{{CONFIRM}}`
package pricing.

### Who owns the site?

You — code, designs, hosting, and analytics transfer to your accounts at launch.

## Recruitment Process Outsourcing

### How is RPO different from a staffing agency?

Agencies fill roles per-placement; RPO is a dedicated team on a predictable cost, working your
process month after month.

## Virtual Assistance

### How is this different from a marketplace VA?

Dedicated to you alone, vetted three ways, trained on your playbook, with a supervisor and a trained
backup.

## Accounting Outsourcing

### Will you replace my CPA?

No — we handle the day-to-day books alongside your CPA, who keeps tax strategy and filings.

## Legal Process Outsourcing

### Is outsourcing legal support ethical?

Yes, with proper attorney supervision and confidentiality — both built into every engagement in
writing. Akrostech is not a law firm and does not give legal advice.
