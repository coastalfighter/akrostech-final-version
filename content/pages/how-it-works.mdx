# From first call to fully ramped

{/* HERO: The Bridge — suspension bridge of light between a U.S. and an Indian skyline; light-cars carry "Requirements", "Shortlist", "Weekly report". */}

Working with Akrostech feels like hiring — because it is. You interview, you approve, you manage. We
handle sourcing, vetting, payroll, and backup coverage. Here's the whole path, with nothing hidden.

## The timeline

- **Day 0 — Discover.** A 30-minute call. We map the role, the tools, the hours, and what "good"
  looks like. You get a written scope and a fixed quote.
- **Days 1–3 — Match.** We shortlist vetted professionals against your spec. You interview the
  finalists and pick.
- **Week 1 — Onboard.** Your person trains on your systems, your playbook, and your communication
  rhythms. `{{CONFIRM}}` exact onboarding timeline.
- **Weeks 2–4 — Ramp.** Daily check-ins at first, then weekly. A supervisor reviews quality
  alongside you.
- **Day 30 and beyond — Scale.** Add hours, add people, add services. Monthly reviews keep quality
  measured, not assumed.

## How we vet

Three gates, no exceptions: **domain skill** (tested, not claimed), **written and spoken English**
(client-facing ready), and **reliability** (references and track record). `{{CONFIRM}}` vetting
details for the client to approve.

## Security, in writing

- NDAs signed before any system access.
- Role-based access controls; least-privilege logins via password manager.
- Confidentiality protocols in every contract — deepest for legal and accounting work.
- Your data stays in your systems. We bring no shadow IT.

## Communication cadence

- **Daily:** async standup notes in your Slack or Teams.
- **Weekly:** 30-minute review — throughput, quality, priorities.
- **Monthly:** KPI review against the targets we set together.
- **Always:** your person is reachable during your business hours.

## Tools

We work in yours: your ATS, your accounting software, your case management, your project tools. If
your team lives in Slack and Asana, so do we.
