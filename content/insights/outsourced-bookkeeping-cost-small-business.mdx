# How much does outsourced bookkeeping cost for a small business?

Outsourced bookkeeping typically costs **a fraction of an in-house hire** — part-time support for a
simple business runs well under $1,000/month with most providers, while full-time dedicated support
costs a fraction of a $50k–$65k U.S. bookkeeper's loaded cost. `{{CONFIRM}}` Akrostech pricing bands.
The price moves with transaction volume, account complexity, and whether you need payroll and close
support — not with your revenue.

> **Key takeaways**
>
> - Pricing follows transaction volume and scope, not revenue.
> - Part-time suits simple books; full-time suits daily transaction flow.
> - Cleanup of behind-books is a separate fixed-price project — get it quoted alone.
> - Compare quotes on scope (what's included weekly/monthly), not just the number.

## What do providers actually charge?

Three common models: **hourly** ($20–$60/hr offshore, $60–$150/hr U.S. — wide ranges, verify per
provider), **flat monthly** by transaction tier (most common for ongoing support), and
**per-resource** (a dedicated part-time or full-time bookkeeper). Akrostech quotes per-resource or
per-project, fixed in writing. `{{CONFIRM}}` bands.

## What moves the price?

1. **Transaction volume** — 50 transactions a month vs. 2,000 is a different job.
2. **Account complexity** — multiple entities, inventory, or project accounting add work.
3. **Payroll** — headcount and pay frequency.
4. **Close and reporting** — a reconciled close by the 10th with management reports costs more than
   bare-bones categorization, and it's worth it.
5. **Cleanup** — behind-by-months books need a one-time cleanup project first.

## The hidden cost of cheap bookkeeping

A suspiciously cheap quote usually excludes reconciliations, close, or review — the parts that make
books trustworthy. Ask every provider: _what exactly happens weekly, what happens monthly, and who
reviews the work?_ Then compare scopes, not prices.

## In-house vs. outsourced: the honest math

A U.S. in-house bookkeeper at $50k–$65k salary costs $65k–$85k loaded. Outsourced part-time support
handles the same workload for many small businesses at a fraction of that — and scales up or down
without hiring cycles. The break-even where in-house wins is high daily volume plus a need for
on-site presence; most businesses under 50 people never reach it.

## Frequently asked questions

### Will outsourced bookkeeping work with my CPA?

Yes — that's the intended setup. Clean, reconciled books make your CPA faster and cheaper at tax
time.

### How do I hand over messy books?

Start with a scoped cleanup: fixed price, defined end date, quoted before work begins. Then move to
monthly support.

### Is my financial data safe with an offshore provider?

Ask about NDAs, access controls, least-privilege logins, and where data lives. Get it in the
contract. (Ours are standard — see [Accounting Outsourcing](/services/accounting-outsourcing).)
