## Program description

Akrostech Consulting LLC may send SMS messages about your inquiry, booking reminders, and — only if
you opt in separately — occasional service updates.

## Opt-in and opt-out

- You opt in by checking the SMS consent box on our forms or by texting START to `{{CONFIRM}}`
  business number.
- **To opt out, reply STOP at any time.** You'll get one confirmation message, then no more.
- For help, reply HELP or contact `{{CONFIRM}}` canonical email.

## Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies (typically 1–4 per month for reminders). Message and data rates may apply
per your mobile plan.
