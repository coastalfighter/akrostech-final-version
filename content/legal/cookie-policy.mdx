# Cookie Policy

**Last updated:** October 12, 2026.

## The short version

We use as few cookies as possible. Nothing for analytics or marketing loads until you say yes in the
cookie banner. You can change your mind anytime.

## What we use

| Cookie               | Purpose                                    | Loads when         |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------ |
| `akr-consent`        | Remembers your cookie choice               | Always (necessary) |
| `akr-announcement`   | Remembers dismissed announcement bars      | Always (necessary) |
| `_ga`, `_ga_*`       | Google Analytics 4 (anonymous usage stats) | After consent      |
| LinkedIn Insight Tag | Measures LinkedIn campaign performance     | After consent      |
| Meta Pixel           | Measures Meta campaign performance         | After consent      |

No advertising cookies load before consent. Analytics is wired through Google Consent Mode v2, so a
"reject" genuinely means no tracking.

## Managing cookies

Use the **Cookie settings** link in the footer to reopen the banner and change your choice. You can
also block cookies in your browser settings — the site keeps working, minus the embedded booking
calendar until you allow its cookies.

## Changes

If we add a new cookie category, we'll ask for consent again.
