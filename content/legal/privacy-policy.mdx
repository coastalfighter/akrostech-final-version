# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** October 12, 2026. _(`{{CONFIRM}}` — client legal review required before launch.)_

Akrostech Consulting LLC ("Akrostech," "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we
collect, why, and what you can do about it.

## What we collect

- **Contact details** you give us: name, email, phone, company, and message content when you use our
  forms or book a call.
- **Application materials** if you apply for a role: CV and contact details.
- **Usage data:** pages visited and device information, collected only after you consent via our
  cookie banner (see Cookie Policy).
- **Newsletter:** your email address, only if you opt in (double opt-in).

## Why we collect it

To respond to inquiries, deliver our services, process applications, and — with consent — understand
how the site is used so we can improve it. We do not sell personal information.

## Cookies and consent

Analytics and marketing tags load only after you accept them in our cookie banner, wired to Google
Consent Mode v2. See our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy).

## Sharing

We share data only with the processors needed to run the site (hosting, email delivery, scheduling,
analytics-after-consent). Every processor is bound by contract. We disclose information if required
by law.

## Your rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your personal data at any time via `{{CONFIRM}}`
canonical contact email. We respond within 30 days.

## Data retention

Inquiry data is kept for 24 months; application materials for 12 months; analytics data per your
consent settings. Backups expire on a rolling 90-day cycle.
