# Tagline Options — M1

Ten options for the client to pick one. All work from the "Across" brand idea (Section 4 of the
brief): Akrostech closes distances — across time zones, across oceans, across the gap between a plan
and a finished result. Voice: confident, warm, precise.

## The ten

1. **Your team, across every hour.**
2. **Talent across oceans. Results on your desk.**
3. **Built across. Delivered here.**
4. **Across time zones. On your team.**
5. **The distance is ours. The results are yours.**
6. **Work that crosses the ocean. Standards that don't.**
7. **Your second shift, across the world.**
8. **Across the map. Inside your workflow.**
9. **While you sleep, we ship.**
10. **One team, two continents, zero distance.**

## Recommendation

**#1 ("Your team, across every hour.")** is the strongest: it names the offer (your team), the
mechanism (across), and the proof (every hour — the time-zone overlap and the overnight handoff). It
also sets up the home page's "24-Hour Handoff" story beat-for-beat.

**#9 ("While you sleep, we ship.")** is the runner-up: the most memorable, and it makes the
follow-the-sun model concrete in five words. Best as a campaign line if #1 becomes the master
tagline.

## Usage once chosen

The picked tagline appears in: the home hero (as the H1's spirit, not necessarily the literal H1 —
the H1 must hold the primary keyword), the closing CTA band rotation, the `og:description` fallback,
and the llms.txt summary. The client can also keep one as a campaign line and one as the master
tagline.
